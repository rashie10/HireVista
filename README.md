# 💼 HireVista — Master Your Next Interview

**HireVista** is an AI-powered mock interview platform that helps you **practice, improve, and ace your next interview** with confidence.  
Whether you’re preparing for tech interviews, HR rounds, or company-specific roles — HireVista provides personalized feedback, analytics, and motivation to help you succeed. 🚀

---

## 🌟 Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Analysis** — Get intelligent feedback on your interview responses using advanced AI.
- 📹 **Video Practice** — Record and review your mock interviews with camera monitoring.
- 📊 **Detailed Analytics** — Track your strengths, weaknesses, and progress over time.
- 🏆 **Achievements & Gamification** — Earn badges and stay motivated throughout your preparation.
- 👥 **User-Friendly Interface** — Simple, responsive, and designed for seamless practice sessions.
